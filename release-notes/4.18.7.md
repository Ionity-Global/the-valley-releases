**THE VALLEY v4.18.7** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

A small update: the launcher shows **UPDATE 4.18.7** and fetches it by itself.

**A greener Karoo**
- About half again as much vegetation across the valley.
- The Karoo's own plants now grow everywhere too: wild rooibos, searsia, Namaqualand daisies and flowers, ice plant, succulents, shrubs and ferns.

**View distance that really saves your PC**
- The DRAW DISTANCE slider now works like in other big games: far-away models switch to simpler versions sooner, the far landscape uses less detail, and at the low end distant textures are softer.
- Creatures and camp folk beyond your view range now sleep — hidden and costing nothing — and wake as you come near. Waves, titans and anything fighting you always stay awake.

**A cleaner welcome**
- The crest on the title screen is no longer cut off at the top - it sits whole, with soft edges, in its true shape.
- The loading screen shows the full crest larger, and a new line of valley wisdom under the bar every few seconds.


**Area spells land where you point**
- Meteor, Starfall, Blizzard, Hurricane, Rain of Fel, Eagle Strike, Volley / Arrow Storm, Hammer of Dawn and every ground channel are now cast on the spot you choose.
- Press the key: a glowing circle follows your mouse on the ground. LEFT-CLICK (or press the key again) to cast there - channels rain on that circle for their full time. RIGHT-CLICK to cancel.
- Beyond the spell's range the circle stops at the edge of your reach.
