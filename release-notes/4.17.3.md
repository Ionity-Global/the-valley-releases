**THE VALLEY v4.17.3** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

- **No more crash at the end of a round** (the "Assertion failed: Bunch" error when the map resets).
- **A new round is a new war:** when the hour turns, every town goes back to its own side (the last round's owner used to keep the map).
- **New players welcome:** creeps no longer jump to your level. A new map starts with low-level creeps that grow slowly with the round (level 4 at the start, +1 every 5 minutes).
- **Fewer units on you:** a side's creep wave only marches out of a town it still owns, nothing spawns within 55 m of an enemy champion, fewer are alive at once, and a creep's call for help brings at most 2 friends (it used to pull a whole town).
- **Half the gold:** town upgrades, troops, patrols, towers, tower enchants and unlearning talents cost about half the gold.
- **Healers carry the light:** a Cleric / Shaman glows at night and lights up the allies around them.
- **7 champion slots**, **score and quests fold away** beside the minimap (with the time), **titans wait** 3–6 minutes and never rise at base camp, **the Valley WIN popup** stays until the reset.
- **Boulders fixed** on the cliffs and roads; **no more glowing white animals** at night.
- **New painted bag icons**, and **sell as many as you like:** Shift + click marks items, then **Sell N**.
- **The launcher:** the full THE VALLEY logo is back, and a **GM MESSAGE** box under CHECK / REFRESH carries news from the team.
