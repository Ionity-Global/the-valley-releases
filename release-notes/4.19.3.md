## The King of the North
- A **level-200 raid boss** on his frozen throne in the far north (about 600, 1110 m), with four Frostbound guards. He never leaves his camp.
- **Frost Smash** (ring at his feet, 1.5 s warning), **Glacier Toss** (a boulder on the farthest champion, 1.6 s warning), **Blizzard Aura** (cold every second within 16 m), **Enrage** below 30 %.
- Every 3 minutes **his host marches south** on the nearest towns: thralls, bowmen and a brute.
- **Legacy of the North**: every kill drops one Legacy piece for the next slot you lack - Frostbite (frost-burst weapon), the Crown (-10 % damage taken), Heart-of-Ice Cuirass (4 % thorns), Stride of the Long Winter (+10 % speed), Heart of Winter (0.3 % health a second) and more. Ice-blue in your bags.
- He returns 30 minutes after he falls. The **Barghest, centaur, griffon and mountain dragon** now stand in the valley.

## Fair fights
- **Creeps are your level** (give or take one), from the first minute, measured against your champion: about five of your blows for a common foe, its blow about 5.5 % of your health. The end of one-shotting everything.
- **PvP level gap**: a player more than 10 levels above you does 75 % less damage to you; more than 20 levels, 90 % less.
- **Captures**: standing anywhere in a town's capture ring counts (you had to stand within 3.5 m of the flag - the bar never moved).
- **Troops press on**: when their route ends they march on the nearest town that is not yours; when every town is yours, to the enemy's landing camp, where they wait outside the gate until someone comes out.
- **The guardians hunt**: strike anyone in a landing camp and its level-999 Commander / Warchief follows you across the valley for 60 seconds.

## Progression
- **Capture a town: +2 levels. Capture an enemy town: +3 levels** (stand in the ring when it falls). Past 100: one Paragon level.
- **Gathering** gives about 4 % of a level per node, **crafting** about 6 %.
- **Blessings last**: the shrine's Sovereign Elixir and the campfire's Potjie Warmth last 30 minutes and survive three deaths.
- **Scroll of Town Portal** (key Y, the blue button above the bar): home to your harbour, free, every 90 seconds. Not in combat.

## The valley
- **Calm shadows**: the day is now one real hour and the sun glides to its place every 45 seconds instead of turning every frame (the jumping shadows and their lag are gone).
- **Nights 25 % lighter.**
- **The chains come off**: the Chainbreaker, Shieldbreaker and Ironjaw no longer drag flailing glowing chains.

## Interface
- **Other players glow purple** in the target frame, with a PLAYER tag.
- **Minimap zoom**: the mouse wheel over the minimap, or its + / - buttons (80 m to 600 m).
- **Bigger match clock** at the top; the realm map's legend text no longer overlaps.
- **.ampies** in the chat console: a massive flame burns every enemy within 40 yards (bosses lose a tenth; two minutes between uses).
