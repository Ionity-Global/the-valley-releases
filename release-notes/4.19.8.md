# The Valley 4.19.8 — "The Old Map"

## The map, redrawn
- The realm map is now an old ink map: rivers, roads and bridges, towns, harbours with ships, camps, landmarks, forests and mountains, with every name lettered on it, a cartouche, a compass and a scale.
- The map still shows what is live: who holds each town, your quests, you, your allies.
- **Town Portal** says CHOOSE LOCATION and pings every place you can open it to on the map.

## A quiet sign-in
- Behind the sign-in, menu and champion forge you now hear only the theme, a soft wind and your clicks. The fights, beasts and voices of the world behind it are silent.
- If loading takes too long, the intro film is skipped instead of starting halfway through your sign-in.

## The Ashclaw walls
- The Ashclaw camp and town walls now stand as real walls: 3.4 m of black stone with iron spikes and skull-crowned pillars. Before, they were giant slabs that blocked the camera.
- The gothic gate is the right size again.

## Bags and the potion shop
- Gman's Brew, Elixir of the Lion, Elixir of the Ionflow and Moonflower Tonic have their own painted flasks. The potion shop and your supplies list show the icon of every item.
- **SELL ALL** in the potion shop sells everything the shop buys in one click.
- **Sell all** in your bags sells every piece of gear. Click it twice; the first click tells you what you would get.
- **Destroy**: mark bag pieces (Shift + click) and press Destroy. Every supply row also has an **X** that throws the stack away. Both ask you to click twice.

## No more endless fights at the base camps
- The faction war parties now gather on the road about 120 m out, stop well short of the enemy camp, march every 2 minutes instead of every 42 seconds, and at most 8 are on the field at once.
