# The Valley 4.19.4 - Clear the Field

**Performance**
- Troops no longer pile up outside the enemy gate. Once every town is taken, only 12 per side wait at the gate; later waves run round the other towns.
- Boss call-ups ("Oproep" soldiers) are capped at 4 near a boss and fall away after 1 to 2.5 minutes. Before, they never left.

**Town Portal**
- Press Y (or the button) and the realm map opens: pick your harbour or any town in the valley. Enemy towns are marked. Every 90 s, not in combat.
- The button now shows a portal swirl icon.

**Titans**
- Titans top out at level 300.
