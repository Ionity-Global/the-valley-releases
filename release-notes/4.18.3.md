**Tower limit**
- Each champion can build at most **7 new towers in any 5 minutes** (build mode and field watchtowers). When you reach the limit the game tells you when the next one is allowed.
- Upgrading or capturing an existing tower does not count toward the limit.
- Fixed: the field watchtower's cost message now shows the real price (45 gold, 70 wood, 60 stone).
