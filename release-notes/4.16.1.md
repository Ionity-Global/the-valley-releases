**THE VALLEY v4.16.1** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

A small patch on top of 4.16.0 (the launcher fetches it by itself).

- **Nobody walks on the spot any more** — a champion, friend or creature that stands still stands still.
- **ROUND plate shows TIME IN MAP and SERVER UP** — how long this round has run and how long the World Server has been up. Servers list their uptime and round.
- **The gate at the turn of the hour is 10 s** (was 25). Joining mid-round never waits: the clock is the server's.
- **Potions regrow:** one Health Potion and one Mana Potion a minute, each only while you carry fewer than 2.
