**THE VALLEY v4.17.0 "SHARED BLOOD"** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

The launcher fetches it by itself. The first step of shared combat:

**One Titan for everyone**
- The World Server decides when and where a Titan rises (every 4–6 minutes near a random player; the Mega Titan every 25 minutes). Every player sees the same Titan at the same place.
- Its health is the server's: every blow from every player counts, and the health bar everyone sees is the same bar.
- The nearest player's game drives the body; the others see it walk, and it still swings at whoever stands in reach.
- When it falls, **everyone who struck it** is paid the Epic (and sometimes Legendary) loot, gold and XP. The LOG tab names the slayer and how many shared the spoils.
- A single blow can take at most 6 % of its health, so a crowd takes it down together.

Only players within 300 m receive the Titan's movement, so the server's traffic stays small.
