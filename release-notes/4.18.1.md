**THE VALLEY v4.18.1** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

- **No more hanging cliff rocks:** before a rock is placed on a terrain wall, the ground under its whole footprint is checked. No rock hangs over a lower step or sticks out far past its wall any more.
- **Line of sight for enemies:** archers and casters no longer shoot or cast through walls, cliffs or buildings. If they can't see you, they move in until they can. The flame ring also needs a clear line.
- **No more walking on air:**
  - You can no longer stand on the very corner of a terrace step with your body over the drop. You still step up anything to 55 cm.
  - Your feet now sit properly on slopes.
  - Creeps and beasts step down off ledges instead of hovering on the edge.
  - Other players are no longer predicted upward into the air. When they walk, their feet are placed on the ground.
- **Paladin tooltips updated:**
  - Judgment: 1.5 sec stun, interrupt, +20% damage taken, 10 sec cooldown.
  - Crusader Strike: 15% chance to strike again, up to 3 extra strikes.
