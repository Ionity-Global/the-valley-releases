**THE VALLEY v4.16.0 "IRON & ALE"** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

The launcher shows **UPDATE 4.16.0** and fetches it by itself (no full download). New players simply run **TheValley_Setup.exe** below. The World Server now needs 4.16 — an older game is told to update.

**Fixed: nobody could join the World Server**
- The game knocked on the server before you had signed in, was refused, and the retry after the sign-in never reconnected. Now it waits for your Valley account and joins by itself — you and your friends finally stand in the same Valley.
- A server that is newer than your game tells you to press UPDATE.

**One shared Valley at war**
- The ten towns belong to the whole server. Stand inside a town's ring with no enemy in it to take it; a bar shows who is taking it and how long is left. Hold every town (all but the landings) for 15 minutes and your side wins; then the map resets.
- Town buffs for your side: Rooiklou Kraal (brewery) +7 % melee dodge, +5 % spell resist · Wesstroom Houtmeule (lumbermill) rangers shoot 10 % faster, melee +7 % · Ysterkrans Steengroef (smithy) +15 % attack power · Sneeuberg Goudmyn gold.
- Orbs on the roads: Madness, Stoneskin, Haste, Clarity, Battle Fury, Second Wind — first touch wins.
- **One clock for everyone:** the server owns the round, the horn, the hour of the day and the weather. Every player hears the horn at the same moment and stands under the same sky. The HUD shows ROUND n.
- A town sends at most 3 reinforcement packs every 2 minutes.

**Social**
- Right-click a player (or click the target portrait): **DUEL · WHISPER · INVITE TO PARTY**.
- A duel challenge pops up in the middle of the screen with ACCEPT / DECLINE and the seconds left.
- Chat has two tabs: **General** and **LOG** — the LOG keeps every kill, capture, titan and server notice.

**Sterkfontein Grotte**
A cave in the lower Valley: ghosts, wailing wraiths and the Ghost of Mrs Ples in a torch-lit chamber, crocodiles in the pool. Clear it and drink from the well: +11 % regeneration for 10 minutes.

**Fixes and feel**
- Titans walk on the ground (they floated and flew).
- Every Fab animal moves again — the cow, zebra, lion, elephant, rhino, fox and the rest stood frozen in the packaged game.
- The female ranger rests her bow between fights.
- The top of the screen (compass, war bar, wave strip, town panel, boss bar) is exactly centred.
- Settings → RESOLUTION really applies (borderless is always your desktop size; a chosen size goes fullscreen).
- EXIT GAME under RESUME in the pause menu — it saves first.
- Warrior: Charge 50 yd, Thunder Clap slows 30 % for 3 s, Shield Wall 15 s and shields allies within 25 m for 10 %.
- The one you talk to stops and faces you; camp gates swing; timber posts; cliff rock on steep walls.
