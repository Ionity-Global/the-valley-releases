# The Valley 4.19.2 "Iron Crown"

(c) 2018-2026 Antwerp Designs | Ionity (Pty) Ltd | AEDI | Policy 986 AED | Building Tomorrow, Today.

Everything since 4.18.7 in one update (4.18.8 - 4.19.2).

## Combat & classes
- **Warlock - Shard Armor** (always on): 15% less damage taken, attackers take 7% of their hit back, 0.5% health regenerated per second.
- **Cleric & Shaman - Thorns** (always on): attackers take 5% of their hit back.
- **Paragon levels** past level 100: far slower, each gives +1 to every stat and +1 attack. Blue level badge, blue XP bar. The valley's foes stop scaling at 100.
- **Aimed Shot** cast time +50%.
- **Ground-targeted area spells** and channels; **5-second spawn shield** after every respawn.
- **Death costs 1 level** (a Paragon level past 100).
- **Faction towers fire on enemy players**.

## Multiplayer
- You now **see other players' attacks and spells**: their swing or cast, the bolt to the target and the impact.
- **Right-click a player's portrait**: Duel / Whisper / Invite. Duel and party invites pop up in the centre of the screen.
- **Chat bubbles** above heads for /s. **Whispers** in bright orange with a chime and a banner.
- **Allies** of your faction on the minimap.

## World
- **The Stone Warden** (Stone Golem) at the Watchstone; the **Vampyr Stalker** hunts at night; a dark stone **monument** with statue and sword.
- **Sterkfontein Grotte**: the ledge and the floating building are fixed.
- A placed tower pushes players aside; nobody rides on a tower's roof any more.
- Bigger, brighter station labels.

## Items & interface
- **Potion vendor shop window**: buy 1/5/10/20, sell what you select. New **Gman's Brew** (+30% speed for 60 s).
- **Hold Shift** over an item to see what you wear in that slot.
- **Type your own champion name** at creation; the attunement tile shows what it gives.
- Mouse-look keeps turning past the screen edge; the mouse stays in the window; **Quit** from the sign-in page.
- Calmer shadows, lighter rain.

The Barghest, centaur, griffon and dragon models follow in 4.19.3 with the King of the North.
