**THE VALLEY v4.15.0 "WILD KAROO"** — Ionity (Pty) Ltd

Install or update with one small file: **TheValley_Setup.exe** (below). It downloads the game itself and never re-downloads what you already have. Every piece is verified when it arrives and every installed file is verified as it is unpacked ("Verifying items ..."), so a damaged file stops the setup instead of breaking the game.

**Play together**
- PvP between players: Kernel against Ashclaw, every blow checked by the World Server.
- Duels: `/duel <name>` — a banner drops, a 20 m ring, 3-2-1, fight to 1 HP. No death, no level lost; the Valley hears who won. Leave the ring for 5 s and you forfeit.
- Line of sight: ranged spells and shots need a clear line — hills, cliffs, walls and buildings block them.
- Rejoins the World Server by itself once you are signed in. Ping shown bottom-right.

**The Wild Karoo**
- Vegetation packs across the whole valley; real zebra, cow, fox and more; photoscanned pebble fields.
- Ore veins with real copper, iron, gold and mana-crystal ore.
- Draw distance by object size (DRAW DISTANCE slider) — big help on weaker PCs.

**Classes**
- Rogues use Energy (starts at 30 of 150, refills 10 a second); Rage builds 1:1 from damage taken and 1:2 from damage dealt.
- New spell effects, casting animations and hit effects; champions talk ("I need more mana!", "Ouch!").
- Towers have less HP and grow stronger for every town your side holds.

**Feel**
- Controls no longer stick: mouse smoothing off, no more micro-freezes on heavy hits, full speed straight after a cast.
- Resolution is a dropdown in settings.
