**THE VALLEY v4.18.2** — Ionity Global (Pty) Ltd

A small update: the launcher shows **UPDATE 4.18.2** and fetches it by itself.

**Warrior**
- Charge: really runs the whole 50 yd now, steering onto the target, untouchable until it lands (it used to stop after about 10 m).
- Thunder Clap: slows enemies by 30 % for 3 sec (and they still take +20 % damage for 5 sec).
- Shield Wall: 30 % less damage for 15 sec, and every ally within 25 m takes 10 % less damage for 15 sec too (the ally shield never switched on before).

**Readable text, whatever your graphics settings**
- Full screen always runs at your monitor's own resolution. A lower Resolution choice now lowers only the 3D world, so the HUD, menus and every letter stay sharp and full size.
- All HUD text is a little larger and never smaller than 13 pixels; light text gets a soft shadow on bright ground.

**Also in this update**
- A new install starts on EPIC graphics at full resolution.
- The northern waves are the UNDEAD: two lanes march south from the north-west and north-east passes — thralls, bowmen, brutes, ghosts, death-witches and a death-lord.
- Every skill tooltip now says what the skill really does; buff icons explain themselves when you hover over them.
- Fixed: the Grotte wraiths and reaper and the kobold slingers now appear with their proper bodies.
